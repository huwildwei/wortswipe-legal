WortSwipe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-07

WortSwipe ("the app") is a German vocabulary learning app. This policy explains what data the app and its third-party services collect and how it's used.

Data stored on your device

WortSwipe has no user accounts and no server-side backend. All learning data is stored locally on your device, in a private app database, and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your vocabulary progress (which words you've seen, spaced-repetition scheduling state)
  • Your practice/streak history and daily activity
  • Your level-assessment exam attempts and scores

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. We have no access to it and cannot recover it.

Advertising

WortSwipe is free to use and shows interstitial ads via Google AdMob, with consent gathered through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) in line with GDPR (EEA), UK, and equivalent consent requirements. Before any ad is requested, the app checks the user's consent status via UMP and only proceeds if ads are permitted.

When an ad is requested, Google (and its advertising partners) may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, screen)
  • App activity relevant to ad delivery (ad requests shown/clicked, frequency capping)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required (EEA/UK), you can control ad personalization consent through the in-app consent form shown by UMP; where not required, Google's platform-level ad settings apply. You can also manage ad personalization globally in your device's Google Settings.

Permissions

The app requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE solely to load ads and check consent status with Google's services — not for any other network activity.

The app also requests RECORD_AUDIO, used only for the optional Voice-Match feature, which listens to your spoken pronunciation of a word during a lesson to give instant feedback. This audio is processed entirely on-device using Android's on-device speech recognizer — it is never transmitted anywhere, never sent to us or to Google, and is not saved or stored in any form; it exists only for the instant of that check. Voice-Match is optional and unavailable on devices that don't support on-device recognition.

Open data credits

WortSwipe's A1 word list is based on patsytau/anki_german_a1_vocab, used under CC BY-SA 4.0 — only the word list was extracted; translations, examples, and audio are generated independently and are not part of the original deck.

Data we do not collect

We (the app developer) do not operate any servers, do not collect analytics, do not use crash reporting, do not require or collect an account, email address, or any other personal identifier, and do not sell data. All data collection described above is performed by Google's AdMob/UMP SDKs, not by app code we control.

Children

WortSwipe is not directed at children and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (e.g. a new SDK is added), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
